Common questions

How much does testing cost?

Every price is published on the quote desk: soil from $9, water from $22, food from $24, feed and forage from $26 and environmental panels from $110 per sample. Orders are billed at a $35 minimum, and quotes stay valid for 30 days.

How fast will I get results?

Each test shows its standard turnaround — most run in 2–7 business days from receipt. Priority service returns results in 3 business days where methods allow, and rush service in 24–48 hours. Confirm rush capacity before shipping.

Do you offer volume discounts?

Yes, automatically: 10% off at 10+ samples, 15% at 25+, 20% at 50+ and 25% at 100+. The quote desk applies the discount as you type your sample count — no negotiating required.

How do I submit samples?

Build your quote online, submit it, then print the combined quote and chain-of-custody form and ship it with your samples. A prepaid sample kit with bottles, bags, ice pack and return label is available as an add-on.

Can I get help interpreting my results?

Add written interpretation to any order and an analyst walks through what the numbers mean and what to do next — useful for soil fertility plans, well-water issues and feed ration decisions.
