# Calculator link-building kit

Heartland's Authority Score is ~2, and "peptide calculator" is a link fight. These are the
moves that earn links to `/peptide-calculator/` and the compound pages specifically.

## Ground rules (these protect the brand and the accounts)

- **Always disclose.** "I work with Heartland Bio Labs" goes in every post and DM. Undisclosed
  vendor posts get removed and the domain gets reported. Reddit spam filters remember domains.
- **Read each subreddit's rules first.** Many peptide subs ban vendor links or source talk
  outright. Where they do, don't post; message the mods instead (template B).
- **Lead with the tool, not the store.** The calculator and embed need no signup, have no
  ads and don't ask for an email. That's the pitch. Never mention prices or discount codes
  in these threads.
- **Never answer dosing questions** in comments, even when asked. Point to the math, not a dose.
- Reddit links are nofollow. Their value is referral traffic, brand searches and
  citations by AI answer engines, which pull heavily from Reddit. Followed links come from
  wikis, blogs and roundups.
