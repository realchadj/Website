## START HERE — the 60-second highlight

The single highest-leverage 60 seconds on the account. Every stranger who lands
on the page and is curious taps this. Shoot it in one take, vertically, in your
"desk" setup. Re-record it each quarter.

Five frames, roughly 12 seconds each.

**Frame 1 — who, and the hook**
> "You probably know me as the villain from The Bachelorette. That was ten
> years ago, and it's the least interesting thing about me now."

**Frame 2 — the fall, briefly and without self-pity**
> "What happened after the show was worse than the show. `[ONE SENTENCE — the
> honest version of the bottom. It does not need to be graphic. It needs to be
> true and specific.]` By `[YEAR]` I was `[WHERE / WHAT STATE]`."

**Frame 3 — the turn**
> "`[THE MOMENT OR DECISION THAT STARTED IT. One sentence.]` I stopped trying
> to get the old life back and started building a different one."

**Frame 4 — what you do now**
> "Now I live in Tulsa, I'm in the best shape of my life, and I build health
> companies. Not as a guy who found a supplement — as a guy who had to rebuild
> from the bottom and paid attention to what actually worked."

**Frame 5 — what this account is for, and the ask**
> "This account is the whole thing, in public. The training, the bloodwork, the
> business, the parts that didn't work. If you're in your thirties or forties
> and you think you've left it too late — you haven't, and I'll show you why.
> Link's in the bio."

**Direction notes.** Talk to the lens, not past it. No music under frames 1–3.
Don't smile in frame 2 and don't perform the pain either — flat delivery on the
hardest line is far more credible than emotion. Caption every frame; most
viewers watch highlights muted.

## The three pinned posts

Together these are a complete funnel that runs whether or not you post that week.
Film all three in one session.

### Pin 1 — the origin reel
**Job:** make them feel something. 45–60 seconds.

Structure: cold open on the hardest line, then chronology.

> **Hook (0:00):** "650,000 articles were written about me in one month. Not one
> of them was about the part that almost killed me."
>
> **Body:** the same arc as the START HERE script, but slower and with detail.
> One specific image per beat — `[WHAT THE APARTMENT LOOKED LIKE]`, `[WHAT YOU
> WEIGHED]`, `[WHAT YOU WERE DOING AT 2PM ON A TUESDAY]`. Specificity is the
> whole thing. "I was in a bad place" is worth nothing; "`[SPECIFIC DETAIL]`" is
> worth the entire post.
>
> **Close:** "I'm not telling you this because it's inspiring. I'm telling you
> because if you're there right now, the way out is boring and it's available."

No CTA on this one. Its only job is that they finish it and follow.

### Pin 2 — the "what I do now" explainer
**Job:** convert feeling into understanding. 30–40 seconds.

> **Hook:** "People keep asking what I actually do now, so here it is in thirty
> seconds."
>
> **Body:** three things, named plainly. `[1 — the telehealth side, described as
> the practitioner relationship, not the product]`. `[2 — the retail side]`.
> `[3 — the wholesale side, who it serves]`. Then: "I'm in Tulsa, it's a real
> company with a real address, and you can come see it."
>
> **Close:** "If you want the version where a licensed provider actually looks
> at your bloodwork first — that's door one in my bio."

**Compliance:** no molecule names, no benefit claims, no needles, no prices.
This post describes a *business*, not a product.

### Pin 3 — the proof post
**Job:** convert understanding into a tap. Carousel, not a reel.

Pick whichever you can evidence best today:

- **Your own bloodwork**, 2019 vs. now, side by side, values visible. Strongest
  option if you have it, because it's you, it's dated, and it's unfakeable.
- **The lab / the building / the team.** Proof that a real company exists.
  Underrated: almost nobody in this space can show a physical address.
- **Customer results**, if you have them documented and consented. Compliance-
  check these before they go up — a testimonial that makes a health claim is
  your claim now, not theirs.

Slide 1 carries the whole post: one number or one image with a five-word caption.
Last slide is the only place a CTA appears.

## Highlight covers

Six covers, one flat colour, one word each, one typeface. Not photos.

| Order | Word |
|---|---|
| 1 | START HERE |
| 2 | THE REBUILD |
| 3 | PROTOCOL |
| 4 | CONSULT |
| 5 | WHOLESALE |
| 6 | TULSA |

Same background colour across all six, same type size, same vertical position.
Six identical covers read as a designed system in half a second. Six photo covers
read as noise, and noise directly under your bio is the most expensive noise on
the page.

Order is priority — left to right is the order people tap, and START HERE has to
be first because it's the only one that answers "should I care."

## The landing page

`landing/index.html`. Static, no build step, no backend — same as everything else
in this repo. Open it and fill in the `CONFIG` block at the top:

| Key | What it needs |
|---|---|
| `consultUrl` | Telehealth intake or booking link |
| `shopUrl` | Retail catalogue |
| `wholesaleUrl` | Practitioner / wholesale application form |
| `emailEndpoint` | Formspree, Basin, Kit, Beehiiv — any of them give you a URL |
| `leadMagnet` | Name a real thing you will actually send |

Any door left blank renders as "soon" and is unclickable, so the page is safe to
deploy before all three exist. Ship it with one door if that's what's ready.

Deploy the same way as the rest of the repo: GitHub Pages from the branch, or
drag the folder into Netlify or Cloudflare Pages. It's one file.

**The email capture is not optional.** It's above the doors on purpose, and it's
the reason the page exists at all rather than a Linktree. Everything else on this
page is rented; the list is the only part you own.

## What "working" looks like by day 30

Not follower growth — expect that flat or slightly down as the reality-TV
audience churns out. That churn is the plan working.

- Reach from non-followers above 40% on your best reels
- Engagement rate off the floor: 0.085% → 0.25%+
- Link taps existing at all, from a baseline of zero
- First 250 email addresses
- At least one wholesale enquiry in the DMs

If reach from non-followers is still under 20% at day 30, the problem is hooks,
not the account. Go back to the fifteen in the audit and film five more.
